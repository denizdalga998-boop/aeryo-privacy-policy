Summary

AERYO does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The game runs entirely on your device. It has no account system, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no in-app purchases and no network access. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device, and you can delete them from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

AERYO is a single-player arrow puzzle game published by ORYVONA GAMES ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or about your privacy in AERYO, write to oryvonagames@gmail.com.

2. Data we do not collect

AERYO does not access, collect, store off-device, transmit or share any of the following: name, e-mail address, phone number, account identifiers, location, contacts, calendar, photos, media, files, camera, microphone, health data, payment information, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, usage or interaction analytics, or crash reports. The app does not ask for any of these and contains no code or third-party service that would do so.

3. Network access

AERYO does not request the Android INTERNET permission and makes no network requests. Everything the game needs is packaged inside the app; it works fully offline, including in airplane mode. The only permission the app requests is VIBRATE, used for the optional haptic feedback you can switch off in Settings.

4. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, AERYO saves a small amount of game data in the app's private storage on your device (Android app preferences). This data never leaves your device and is not backed up to any server by the app (Android backup is disabled for AERYO).

WhatWhy
Campaign progress (completed levels, current level, "Perfect Flow" marks, the state of the level you are playing)So the game can continue and show your progress on the Levels screen
Daily Flow progress (which days of a month you completed)To show the monthly calendar and Flow Seal
Settings: sound, vibration, language, theme, and whether the short in-game tips have been shownTo remember your preferences
A small internal play-session record: the start time and last activity time of your current play session (as a timestamp), and a counter of levels completed since the session beganUsed only by the game's own pacing logic on the device (this version shows no advertising; the record is never sent anywhere)

None of this data identifies you as a person, and none of it leaves your device.

5. Data sharing

We do not share any data with anyone, because the app does not collect any. No third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, or account SDKs are used, and there are no social features. The app is built with standard application frameworks (Capacitor and Android Jetpack/AndroidX libraries), which run entirely on your device and do not transmit data.

Note: if you install AERYO from Google Play, Google may process data about the download, your device and any crashes according to Google's own privacy policy and your Google account settings. That processing is performed by Google Play, not by AERYO, and we receive at most aggregated, anonymous statistics from Google Play Console.

6. Retention and deletion

Your locally stored game data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can delete it at any time:

  • inside the game: Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete local game data, which removes all progress and settings; or
  • by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the Android system settings.

Because nothing is stored on our side, there is no data for us to delete or to hand over on request.

7. Children

AERYO is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no data at all, no personal information about any user, of any age, is received by us.

8. Your rights

Under data protection laws such as the GDPR or the Turkish KVKK you may have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection. Since AERYO processes no personal data on our side, these rights are satisfied by the on-device controls described in section 6. You can still contact us at oryvonagames@gmail.com with any privacy question.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of AERYO changes how data is handled (for example if a version ever adds an online feature), we will update this policy, change the effective date at the top, and reflect the change in the app's Google Play listing before that version is released. The current version of this policy is always available at the address linked from the app and from the Google Play listing.
