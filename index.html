Summary

AERYO is a free, single-player puzzle game. It is supported by advertising served by Google AdMob. ORYVONA GAMES has no accounts, no servers and no analytics of its own, and does not receive your personal data. To show ads, however, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app sends certain device and usage information to Google, as described in section 3. Where the law requires it, you are asked for your choices before any ad is requested, and you can change them later in Settings. Your game progress stays on your device. The game works fully offline; without a connection it simply shows no ads.

1. Who we are

AERYO is published by ORYVONA GAMES ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy or about your privacy in AERYO, write to oryvonagames@gmail.com.

2. Advertising in AERYO

  • Full-screen ads between levels. Occasionally, and only when a campaign level has been completed, a full-screen ad may appear before the next level. There are no such ads in the first 15 campaign levels, in Daily Flow, at app start, or during play.
  • Optional rewarded ads. When you run out of Focus, you can choose to watch a short ad to get 1 Focus back. This is always optional: the free Restart is always offered.
  • Ad provider. Ads are served only by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK for consent. No other ad network, mediation, analytics or tracking service is included in the app.
  • Ad content. AERYO asks Google to limit ads to content suitable for general audiences (Google's "G" maximum ad content rating), in line with the game's own "Everyone" content rating.

4. Consent and your privacy choices

Each time AERYO starts, it checks with Google's User Messaging Platform whether a consent message is required where you are (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). If it is required, Google's consent message is shown before any ad is requested, and ads are requested only if your choices allow it. Depending on your choices, Google may show personalized ads, non-personalized ads, limited ads, or no ads. If the consent message cannot be loaded (for example when you are offline), AERYO does not request ads and you keep playing normally.

Where Google indicates that a way to change your choices must be available, AERYO shows a Privacy choices item in Settings. It reopens Google's consent message so you can review or withdraw your choices at any time. Your consent choices are stored on your device by Google's SDK.

You can also:

  • reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your device settings (on most devices: Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads);
  • manage ad personalization for your Google Account in My Ad Center;
  • play offline — without a network connection AERYO requests no ads.

6. What AERYO does not do

AERYO has no account or sign-in, no in-app purchases, no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own, no social features, and no chat. It does not access your location (GPS), contacts, calendar, photos or files, camera or microphone, and it does not ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number. ORYVONA GAMES does not sell your personal data.

8. Children

AERYO is a puzzle game made for a general audience; it is not designed for young children. The age groups the app is intended for are declared in its Google Play listing, and this section will be updated to match that declaration. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we ask Google to show only ads suitable for general audiences. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used AERYO in a way that raises a privacy concern, contact us at oryvonagames@gmail.com; uninstalling the app stops all ad-related processing on the device.

9. Sharing and international processing

The only third party that receives data from AERYO is Google, as the ad provider described above. Google may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under its own safeguards. We do not share or sell data to anyone else.

10. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: Settings › Privacy & Data › Delete local game data removes your progress and settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes all app data, including the consent information stored by Google's SDK.
  • At Google: data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is retained by Google according to Google's policies; you can manage it with the controls in section 4 and in your Google Account.
  • At ORYVONA GAMES: we store no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over.

11. Your rights

Under laws such as the GDPR and the Turkish KVKK you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent (via Privacy choices in Settings). For data processed by Google, you can exercise these rights with Google directly. For any question, contact us at oryvonagames@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Changes to this policy

If a future version of AERYO changes how data is handled (for example, by adding in-app purchases), we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released. The current policy is always available at the address linked from the app and from the Google Play listing.
